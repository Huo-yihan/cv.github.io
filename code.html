关于我 · About Me

我是一名地理信息科学专业的本科生，就读于南京信息工程大学（双一流学科）。我对GIS时空分析、生态环境遥感评估以及多因子建模具有浓厚兴趣。在本科期间，我以第一负责人身份完成了多项科研项目和学科竞赛，积累了丰富的实践经验。我熟练掌握ArcGIS、Supermap、MapGIS等GIS软件，以及R、Python、MATLAB等编程语言，能够独立完成数据处理、空间分析和建模工作。

I am an undergraduate student in Geographic Information Science at Nanjing University of Information Science & Technology (a Double First-Class discipline). I have a strong interest in GIS spatiotemporal analysis, remote sensing for ecological assessment, and multi-factor modeling. During my undergraduate studies, I served as the principal investigator for multiple research projects and academic competitions, gaining extensive practical experience. I am proficient in ArcGIS, Supermap, MapGIS, as well as programming languages such as R, Python, and MATLAB, enabling me to independently conduct data processing, spatial analysis, and modeling.


我的研究兴趣主要集中在干旱区生态环境评估、土壤侵蚀时空变化归因以及气候变化对水资源的影响。我曾基于CMIP6模型对和田河流域进行多因子生态分析，并利用SPI指数研究黄河流域的干旱时空演变。目前，我正在完成题为“重庆市土壤侵蚀时空变化与归因”的毕业论文，旨在为区域水土保持提供科学依据。

My research interests focus on ecological assessment in arid areas, attribution of spatiotemporal changes in soil erosion, and the impact of climate change on water resources. I have conducted multi-factor ecological analysis of the Hetian River Basin based on CMIP6 models and studied drought spatiotemporal evolution in the Yellow River Basin using the SPI index. Currently, I am completing my undergraduate thesis titled "Spatiotemporal Changes and Attribution of Soil Erosion in Chongqing," aiming to provide scientific evidence for regional soil and water conservation.


除了学术研究，我积极参与课外活动与志愿服务。我曾担任游泳协会秘书部部长，组织急救知识普及活动；作为Supermap校园大使，推广GIS竞赛；还担任学生助理，负责多媒体设备维护，累计志愿服务时长超过200小时。这些经历锻炼了我的组织协调能力和团队合作精神。

Beyond academic research, I actively participate in extracurricular activities and volunteer work. I served as Secretary of the Swimming Association, organizing first-aid knowledge activities; acted as Supermap Campus Ambassador, promoting GIS competitions; and worked as a student assistant maintaining multimedia equipment, accumulating over 200 volunteer hours. These experiences have honed my organizational and teamwork skills.
