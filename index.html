关于我 · About Me

我是一名草学专业的研究生，目前就读于兰州大学草学院农经所。我的研究方向聚焦于草地产权制度对生态系统的传导效应，探究产权安排如何影响草地利用方式及其生态后果。

I am a graduate student in Grassland Science at the Institute of Agricultural Economics, College of Grassland Science, Lanzhou University. My research focuses on the transmission effects of grassland property rights on ecosystems, exploring how property arrangements influence grassland utilization patterns and their ecological consequences.

我于2021年在南京信息工程大学获得地理信息科学学士学位（理学），本科期间主修生态环境遥感评估及多因子建模，为当前研究奠定了坚实的技术基础。

I received my B.S. in Geographic Information Science from Nanjing University of Information Science & Technology in 2021, with a focus on remote sensing for ecological assessment and multi-factor modeling, which laid a solid technical foundation for my current research.

我的研究领域涵盖生态环境评估、时空变化归因、地理建模以及机器学习的应用。在本科阶段，我以第一负责人身份完成了多项科研项目和学科竞赛，积累了丰富的实践经验。我曾基于CMIP6模型对和田河流域进行多因子生态分析，并利用SPI指数研究黄河流域的干旱时空演变。我的本科毕业论文题为“重庆市土壤侵蚀时空变化与归因”，旨在为区域水土保持提供科学依据。

My research interests span ecological assessment, spatiotemporal attribution, geographic modeling, and applications of machine learning. During my undergraduate studies, I served as principal investigator for multiple research projects and competitions, gaining extensive hands-on experience. I conducted a multi-factor ecological analysis of the Hetian River Basin using CMIP6 models, and studied drought evolution in the Yellow River Basin with the SPI index. My bachelor's thesis, "Spatiotemporal Changes and Attribution of Soil Erosion in Chongqing," aimed to provide scientific support for regional soil and water conservation.

在研究生阶段，我进一步拓展了研究方向，聚焦于光伏电站建设对粮食生产的影响，运用因果推断方法识别其效应并解析潜在路径。此外，我还以第一著作人身份在畜牧管理算法设计及光伏板下作物识别方向发表软著与专利。

In my graduate studies, I have expanded my research to examine the impact of photovoltaic power plant construction on grain production, using causal inference methods to identify effects and potential pathways. I have also published software copyrights and patents as the first author in the areas of livestock management algorithm design and crop identification under photovoltaic panels.

我在研究工作中具备扎实的技术专长，熟练掌握ArcGIS、Supermap、MapGIS等GIS软件，以及R、Python、MATLAB等编程语言，能够独立完成数据处理、空间分析和建模工作。同时，我熟悉Origin、PowerPoint、Photoshop等制图与展示工具，地图作品曾多次获奖。语言方面，我已通过CET-6，目前正在备考雅思，并具备日语的基础知识。

I bring strong technical expertise to my research, with proficiency in GIS software such as ArcGIS, Supermap, and MapGIS, as well as programming languages including R, Python, and MATLAB, enabling independent data processing, spatial analysis, and modeling. I am also familiar with visualization tools like Origin, PowerPoint, and Photoshop, and my cartographic works have received multiple awards. I have passed CET-6, am preparing for IELTS, and possess basic knowledge of Japanese.

除了学术研究，我积极参与课外活动与志愿服务。我曾担任游泳协会秘书部部长，组织急救知识普及活动；作为Supermap校园大使，推广GIS竞赛；还担任学生助理，负责设备维护，累计志愿服务时长超过200小时。这些经历锻炼了我的组织协调能力和团队合作精神。

Beyond academic research, I actively participate in extracurricular activities and volunteer work. I served as Secretary of the Swimming Association, organizing first-aid knowledge activities; acted as Supermap Campus Ambassador, promoting GIS competitions; and worked as a student assistant maintaining equipment, accumulating over 200 volunteer hours. These experiences have honed my organizational and teamwork skills.
