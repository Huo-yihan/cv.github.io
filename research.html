研究项目 · Research Projects

基于CMIP6模型的和田河流域生态环境多因子评估
第一负责人，大学生创新创业项目，2023.06 – 2024.06
Principal Investigator, Undergraduate Innovation Program, Jun 2023 – Jun 2024

对西北和田河流域干旱区进行多因子生态分析，工作内容包括配置多因子长时段遥感数据，通过相关GIS分析计算土壤侵蚀模数；使用R语言，利用地理探测器源代码，在此基础上进行修改，分析自然因子之间的相关性。以唯一著作权发表软著一项《基于航天遥感的地理分析软件》，并顺利结项。

Conducted multi-factor ecological analysis in the arid area of the Hetian River Basin in Northwest China. Tasks included configuring long-term multi-factor remote sensing data, calculating soil erosion modulus using GIS analysis; using R language to modify the geographical detector source code to analyze correlations among natural factors. Published a software copyright "Space-based Remote Sensing Geographic Analysis Software" as sole author and successfully completed the project.


基于SPI指数的黄河流域干旱时空分析
第一负责人，“共享杯”科技资源共享服务创新大赛论文，2023.08 – 2023.10
Principal Investigator, "Shared Cup" Innovation Competition Paper, Aug 2023 – Oct 2023

基于2000-2020年黄河流域降水数据，运用SPI指数、Mann-Kendall检验和Morlet小波分析，研究干旱时空演变特征。

Based on precipitation data from 2000 to 2020 in the Yellow River Basin, used SPI, Mann-Kendall test, and Morlet wavelet analysis to study drought spatiotemporal evolution.


重庆市土壤侵蚀时空变化与归因
毕业设计（在研），2024.12 – 2025.02
Undergraduate Thesis (ongoing), Dec 2024 – Feb 2025

对重庆市各分区进行土壤侵蚀计算，评估因子影响力，分级分析并对比已有研究。目前论文初稿已完成。

Calculated soil erosion for each subregion of Chongqing, evaluated factor influence, conducted graded analysis and compared with existing studies. First draft of the paper has been completed.
